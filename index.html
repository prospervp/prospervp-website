Questions & answers

Good to know.

What is a Fractional VP?

An experienced executive who works with your business part time, giving you high-level leadership and direction without the cost of a full-time hire.

How can I tell if my business needs one?

If you're juggling too many roles, growth has stalled, or you're unsure how to line up day-to-day work with long-term goals, a Fractional VP can help. The free scorecard is a quick way to check.

How is this different from hiring a full-time VP?

A full-time VP is costly and often more than a small business needs. We give you the same experience part time, and you can scale up or down as your needs change.

Why does a business owner need a coach?

Professional athletes have coaches and top executives have mentors. The most successful owners don't try to have every answer themselves. They have a trusted advisor in their corner.
